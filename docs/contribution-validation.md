## Root causes and bounded changes

The forensic MARGEN diagnosis established improved typography, hierarchy and product
explanation, with less improvement in advanced finish. The changes target three causes:

1. Composition/task relevance was mistaken for sufficient creative evidence. Retrieval
   now separates lexical task match from mechanism contribution and its evidence strength.
2. Loaded finish guidance was applied mainly as corrective QA. Existing finish guidance
   now asks for the highest-leverage credible intervention visible in the artifact, and
   whether an important selected reference relationship survived its adaptation.
3. The first credible native treatment closed visual-material opportunity too early.
   Substantial high-ambition creation now considers a specific material benefit before
   closing the direction; Asset Direction can investigate that opportunity before an
   asset is already declared essential. Generation remains conditional on a credible job.

No new methodology, source, role checklist, always-loaded text, critic, model call,
mandatory effect, artifact or final phase was added. These craft decisions remain
aesthetic heuristics and primary-session responsibilities, not empirical quality rules.

## Retrieval behavior

Compact candidates expose:

- `task_match.observed` and `metadata_only`: matched query terms, explicitly lexical.
- `mechanisms`: concrete role, visible cause and reasoned effect; the relevant two
  are disclosed, with full observations deferred to selected hydration.
- Per-mechanism `evidence`: `clear` or `limited` with an inspected basis; absent
  assessments are `unassessed`, never inferred from confidence, resolution or prestige.
- Image dimensions, actual inspection method and known limitations.
- For a requested role, `contribution_match`: matched/unmatched terms and candidate,
  partial or absent support. This is not a creative-coverage verdict.

`product` no longer expands into commerce/shop/retail. Framing variants are recognized.
A compound relationship must be supported within one mechanism; unrelated mechanisms
cannot pool words to imply support. Partial candidates remain retrievable, but
complementary retrieval now leaves `gap: true` for partial matches and limited evidence.
`gap: false` means a candidate exists, never that the design's evidence is complete.
Ambiguous requests such as “product presentation” still need primary interpretation:
software framing, physical imagery, merchandising and storytelling are different needs.

For role retrieval, candidate search includes both task and mechanism terms. Mechanism
support and assessed clarity precede task similarity, including before the 24-candidate
complementary shortlist. The two axes are disclosed separately rather than collapsed
into a score. Provider labels, family tags and limitation text cannot establish a
mechanism. Exact cause/effect repeats and reused references remain visible; paraphrased
repetition and actual creative contribution require visual judgment.

The existing single-role search and optional complementary command handle visible gaps.
The primary model translates an observed relationship into the search; no new routing
system or automatic visual classifier was introduced. A product frame that needs spatial
integration can prompt framing/layering retrieval without restarting software research.

## Answers to the release questions

**A. Why is the MARGEN set not automatically complete?** Typography, software and
composition describe task fit and some contributions. Each intended visual relationship
still needs adequate visible support; the new output exposes partial matches and preview
limits, and no role list closes creative coverage.

**B. How is under-finish noticed without a premium preset?** Inspect resolution of the
chosen language and survival of materially selected mechanisms. A visible, consequential
gap can justify action even without defects; flatness and no intervention remain valid.

**C. How does one opportunity stay bounded?** Investigate the highest-leverage supported
relationship only when evidence would change a decision, reuse existing context, then
apply or reject and reinspect. No credible benefit means no investigation; subjective
alternatives and speculative novelty do not justify continued work.

**D. Why escape a safe native default?** Material sufficiency is judged against the thesis
before construction convenience closes it. A contextual asset or richer native relationship
can be considered without already being mandatory; precise native UI remains editable,
and both assets and effects must earn their place in the actual composition.
