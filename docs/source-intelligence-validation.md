# Source and reference intelligence correction

Verified 2026-09-05. Development evidence, excluded from the reusable package.
This correction changes source authority, candidate admission and evidence handling.
It does not claim a new six-benchmark design-quality pass or human acceptance.

## Implementation and boundaries

- Recent is canonical. `godly.design` is inactive; `godly.website` resolves only
  historical retained provenance. Old preference identities are retired at read time,
  without rewriting preferences, receipts or images. Explicit empty/replaced pools survive.
- The 23 sources carry tier, specialties, allowed/inappropriate roles and acquisition
  limitations. Eight Tier 1 sources lead general expressive discovery; thirteen Tier 2
  specialists can lead a matching decision; two Tier 3 sources supply qualified gaps.
  Rebrand retains its existing conditional specialist-pool route.
- Source discovery ranks authority before semantic overlap. Retained mechanism retrieval
  ranks demonstrated evidence adequacy and authority before semantic similarity. Industry
  labels cannot promote Tier 3 above otherwise suitable higher-tier evidence.
- Per-mechanism quality and scope are optional persistent observations. `--ambition high`
  admits only explicitly assessed high-quality mechanisms with clear, scoped evidence.
  Ordinary useful references remain available. A confidence label is not a quality gate.
  These are primary-model judgments, not automated visual-quality certification.
- `--evidence-scope` constrains the actual mechanism. A section URL cannot receive page
  or full-site scope. Siteinspire retrieval requires evidence of Selected membership.
  Sibling shots remain separately inspected records; complementary retrieval reports
  reused references/projects and leaves unsupported dimensions as gaps.
- GIF/WebP/APNG frame extraction spans the decoded sequence. Its output explicitly says
  it has not been viewed. Static contact sheets label animated assets first-frame-only.
  Motion admission requires actual primary inspection plus ordered states, trigger,
  continuity, spatial relation, purpose, transferable mechanism, repetition and timing basis.
  Legacy motion-method labels without sequence observations no longer satisfy motion search.
  Link-only video bookmarks preserve structured observations and prior notes. No browser
  automation or validator can establish that the model actually watched enough material.
- Cross-domain transfer remains mechanism-led. Spatial storytelling, product presentation,
  imagery, typography, identity, motion and finish are conditional coverage questions, not
  a mandatory source checklist or a fixed reference count.
- Composition guidance diagnoses uniform containment without banning legitimate grouping,
  tables or Auto Layout. Shared identity permits different local compositions. Product
  presentation separates the native editable UI master from optional device/environment
  imagery. Exact perspective compositing needs an available suitable tool; a planar image
  fill alone does not supply a perspective transform. Preserve the master, inspect the
  composite and disclose any raster derivative. No generated readable replacement UI.

exercise behavior, not wording matches: quality versus confidence/tier, scope, independent
sibling admission, Tier 3 keyword dominance, Tier 2 typography authority, Selected
membership, animated GIF and WebP, decoded frame pixels, stale motion receipts, structured
video bookmarks and restrictive scopes. An intermediate fixture leaked a preceding GIF
into the WebP subcase; the assertion now checks that specific reference. No failing case
was dropped. A static mechanism cannot bypass motion verification with a motion-sequence
scope. Existing activation/routing, incomplete-input, missing-tool and revision probes
remain in the suite; they do not prove fresh automatic skill activation.

## Runtime, installation and limitations

The core source correction grew from 675 to 694 o200k_base tokens. Initial scripted
shadow/form/table routes added 19 tokens; broad landing/ambitious-landing routes added
1,263, and expressive-assets added 1,610. These include conditional guidance and changed
retrieval payloads. They are illustrative emitted-text routes, not active-model tokenizer
parity, image costs or observed activation traces. No additional mandatory network calls,
runtime Python dependencies, agents, integrations or source phases were added.

A separate concurrent task, **Add foundation-first design system**, subsequently changed
shared core/Figma guidance and the package version to 0.6.0. Its changes are preserved;
the 19-token measurement isolates this correction before that edit. Final combined build
verification belongs in the installation record in `validation.md`.

Known limits: no retained corpus item was automatically relabeled elite; high-ambition
retrieval may expose honest gaps until actual items are reassessed. Scope/quality/sequence
records are attestations, not proof of cognition. Full videos across all sources, future
site access/permissions, native device compositing, and the six live Figma benchmarks
were not validated by this correction. No existing Figma design was changed. Human
acceptance and a general improvement in final design quality remain unclaimed.
