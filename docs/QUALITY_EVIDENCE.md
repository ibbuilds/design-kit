## Official model guidance checked September 29

- [GPT-6.1 Sol](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6.1-sol): complex coding/computer use/professional work at lower cost, low/medium/high/xhigh/max effort with medium default; no none/minimal; tool calling uses Responses. API specifications do not establish Codex subscription hours or percentages.
- [GPT-6 family guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/latest-model?model=gpt-6.1-sol): outcome completion, prior authorization, user-over-skill priority and calibrated testing. Its behavioral observations concern Astra; the guide proposes the prompts as starting points across the family and calls for evaluation on the chosen workload. We do not present them as a controlled Sol design benchmark.
- [Reasoning guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/reasoning): effort changes the reasoning allocation; it is not the primary way to recover quality. Verification criteria and access to the right tools matter. Preserved reasoning and cache behavior are API/runtime features, not something a Markdown installer configures.

## Earlier-model guidance used as design principles

- [Frontend prompt instructions](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/frontend-prompt.md) explicitly target GPT-5.5. Useful transferable principles: respect an existing system, match application density to the domain, build the actual experience, use meaningful assets, complete controls/states and inspect responsive output. Its exact radius, letter-spacing, palette and hero prohibitions are sample prompt choices, not universal design laws; copying them would conflict with user-provided designs and the kit's product-specific purpose.
- [GPT-5.6 prompting guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/prompt-guidance-gpt-5p6.md): outcome, evidence, completion criteria, selective tools and removal of repeated instructions. Its internal evaluation percentages are not estimates for GPT-6.1 Sol, this kit or the user's quota. We adopt the lean-contract approach and preserve necessary invariants rather than copying every process step.

## Changes these findings support

1. Keep one compact frontend entrypoint and conditional references instead of injecting the human manual or every workflow.
2. Define a visual direction through product/task, composition, type/content, asset, interaction/mobile and reference contribution. A source must lead to a rendered decision, not remain a bibliography.
3. Distinguish no-base, structural, final-design and accepted-system work; marketing composition and working-application density serve different jobs.
4. Complete authorized work without inventing approval gates. Preserve explicit user stops, choices, budgets and publication instructions.
5. Close on required behavior and material visual quality, with focused verification and changed hypotheses after repeated failure. A two-round ceiling cannot establish readiness; unlimited polishing cannot establish value.
6. Optimize total consumption through the agreed result, including rework. Preserve the user's selected model/settings; model-specific observations are conditional evidence, not the kit's default choice. Higher effort is not a universal visual-quality guarantee.

Reference-provider access policies were inherited from the supplied September 26 guide; third-party MCPs were not installed or re-audited during this update. The curated catalog remains intact. Offline tests validate installation, routing persistence and package integrity, not the design decisions an LLM will make on a future project.

## October 5: observed workflow failure and revised contract

The supplied existing-library conversation exposed an omitted source-to-base comparison, misplaced emphasis on density/widths, overextended interpretation of a preference answer and continued edits after a rejected diagnosis. The agent subsequently confirmed it had read design documentation but had not compared against the named products or researched through MCP. This supports tightening readiness/acceptance, not assuming that every existing system is approved or making a project's aesthetic universal.

A later supplied trace showed a second failure: the agent declared the desired identity from existing design prose and a previously accepted component batch, then captured 34 catalog pages before asking the owner to confirm that reading. The owner supplied a more specific direction and an example, and requested additional MCP research and adaptation into the design documents/system/UI. Later reported reference searches succeeded; optional live capture used a browser fallback. The revision makes the document's aesthetic a hypothesis for open improvement, distinguishes scoped implementation authority from intended visual direction, treats exploratory examples as discovery seeds, and ties accepted relationships to canonical design-document and rendered UI changes. The source project's particular aesthetic is not a universal rule.

The owner's subsequent instruction establishes a discovery default of 4–8 strong, distinct inspected references and a reference-review choice between advancing, refining research and correcting the selection/direction. This is the requested interaction contract, not a research-derived quality threshold or required number of MCP calls. Explicitly bounded faithful/local work can reuse accepted evidence; insufficient coverage must be disclosed rather than padded. These pasted exchanges are maintenance evidence, not authorization to execute the source project's tasks.

### Public methods compared

- [Refero's design skill](https://github.com/referodesign/refero_skill/blob/master/skills/refero-design/SKILL.md) requires research, an explicit visual target and source-linked decisions before implementation, followed by comparison to that target. Its style/screen/flow separation informs evidence selection; its fixed research counts and paid connection are not adopted.
- [Mobbin's redesign skill](https://github.com/mobbin/skills/blob/main/skills/redesign-screen/SKILL.md) scopes problems, preserves the baseline, locks references and ties each change to a visible decision before editing. Its [search skill](https://github.com/mobbin/skills/blob/main/skills/search/SKILL.md) distinguishes app screens, website sections and flows. Its structure-first/unchanged-system policy is narrower than this kit's explicitly authorized visual evolution; it is not copied wholesale.
- [Appllama's improvement playbook](https://github.com/Appllama/appllama-skills/blob/main/skills/appllama-usage/references/improve-a-screen.md) targets research at diagnosed deficits and compares the implementation with real screen references. Its mobile-specific checks, large reference boards, credit model and open-ended perfection loop are not defaults here.
- [Inspo's MCP documentation](https://github.com/Nutlope/inspo/blob/main/apps/mcp/README.md) places guidance in server instructions and recommendation output alongside screenshots and extracted design context. This is evidence delivery, not proof that a standalone skill or its examples outperform alternatives. It remains outside the selected provider setup.
- [Impeccable's skill](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/main/skill/SKILL.src.md) separates actual visual authority from filenames and loads project context selectively. Its [live mode](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/main/skill/reference/live.md) explicitly records observed identity before variation. Its custom runtime/hooks and universal aesthetic rules are not added.

These public procedures were inspected, not installed or benchmarked. The useful shared mechanism is evidence tied to a scoped decision, with an actual rendered comparison. None establishes a numerical improvement probability for Design Kit.

### Headway video

Read the complete publisher transcript for [DESIGN.md vs. MCP: Which One Your Agent Actually Needs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lNAG_ezUzAI), published as [What DESIGN.md Actually Solves for Design Systems](https://www.headway.io/intent-and-craft/what-designmd-actually-solves-for-design-systems). The publisher page embeds the same video ID. YouTube's public caption request returned empty content; the publisher transcript covers 00:00 through the 40:26 closing.

At 05:08-08:18, the speakers recommend essential tokens and only needed usage context. At 10:39-17:53, they distinguish portable system intent from component API retrieval and discuss routing/chunking context. At 23:12, they flag regeneration of components as a potential misuse when a real library already exists. At 30:00 onward, they discuss contributions and governance. These are practitioner accounts, not controlled aesthetic benchmarks. Their discussion of Atlassian does not establish its results independently.

Applied here: inspect real component APIs/assets alongside system intent, preserve one canonical definition of values, add scoped usage/exclusion rules and review changes before propagation. Do not migrate every project's DESIGN.md to Google's format, duplicate its library into prose or mandate a new design-system MCP. The inspiration MCPs selected here are a different capability from a project's own component API service.

### Verification boundary

Native Awwwards returned a preview and source. Direct One Page Love failed with `Unexpected response type`, including text-only mode, although direct endpoint initialization/list/search succeeded. Its response included fractional `annotations.priority`. An ephemeral offline probe through the installed Codex 0.160.0 binary isolated the trigger: decimal `0.9` and `1.0` failed; integer `1` and omitted priority passed. This matches [the reported parser defect](https://github.com/openai/codex/issues/38979).

The Codex-only compatibility adapter removes floating-point content priority metadata. A live query through the installed binary and adapter returned the original/gallery URLs and a JPEG; its 400 × 300 preview was inspected without a model call. After reload, native calls in this desktop chat returned images and links from both selected MCPs, verifying this surface's query/image path. Other hosts and future versions remain separate checks. Offline tests verify preservation and safe config migration, not aesthetic quality. [Behavioral regression trials](WORKFLOW_EVALUATION.md) specify reviewable agent actions; protocol/package tests are not a passed model trial. The revised skill has not yet demonstrated aesthetic uplift in a controlled comparison.
