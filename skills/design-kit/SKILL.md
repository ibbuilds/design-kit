# Design Kit

The intended goal is to maximize final design quality through authoritative UX
knowledge, exceptional visual references, Figma-native creation, critique, and
repeated human feedback across projects and platforms.

This release establishes the skill entry point only. The research, creation,
critique, and iteration workflows and Figma integration are not implemented.
When invoked, make that status clear; do not present those workflows as available
or claim that a Figma document was created or changed.

Keep project-specific context in the user's project, outside this reusable skill.
