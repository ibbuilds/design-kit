# Observe, diagnose, correct

Judge the requested scope against the brief, human decisions, necessary content/UX,
selected reference mechanisms and relevant craft. The primary session judges.
Geometry complements a current render; it cannot prove visual quality. Follow
[Figma's fresh-evidence rule](figma.md) after every meaningful change batch.

Inspect overall hierarchy/rhythm, then important frames individually at readable
scale. Check composition, type relationships, real content, crops, contrast, navigation,
state meaning and construction where relevant. Catch overlap, clipping, broken mobile,
placeholder residue and generic first-pass composition. A thumbnail cannot certify
body text; a hero cannot establish a whole experience. Static Figma cannot certify
runtime keyboard behavior, screen readers or unseen motion.

Reason: observed location/state → concrete cause → bounded intervention → expected
benefit → fresh visual comparison. Preserve strengths and human edits. Compare
equivalent regions/density with references; transfer mechanisms, not identities.
Fix structure before decorating, and relationships before 1px nudges.

| Finding | Action |
|---|---|
| Objective/material defect | Fix within authorized scope and recheck the region. |
| Strong improvement hypothesis | Try the targeted change; keep only an observed improvement. |
| Subjective alternate taste | Stop or let the user choose; do not churn a strong direction. |
| Intentional human decision | Preserve unless asked to reconsider it. |

Stop when no material defect or credible improvement remains, two major attempts fail,
the decision needs human taste, or the user stops. Report blocked visual claims when
fresh rendering is unavailable. Technical validity, policy compliance, specialist
agreement or arbitrary scores do not establish quality.
