# Foundations

Original synthesis; source IDs resolve in [registry](../sources.json). Verified
2026-09-04. Research informs choices; standards set requirements; taste stays explicit.

## Task before surface

- **Principle / problem:** Organize around the person's real decision or action;
  feature inventories and decorative dashboards can hide the job.
- **Use:** New IA, prioritization, mixed-intent products; classify each surface.
- **Do not use:** Invented personas or “best practice” to displace observed tasks.
- **Alternatives:** Task walkthroughs, existing workflow evidence, reversible assumptions.
- **Exceptions:** Safety-critical expert work needs domain validation; exploration
  alone is insufficient training. Persuasion and expressive experiences may lead
  with narrative when that serves the agreed goal.
- **Accessibility:** Include users with varied input, cognition, vision and expertise.
- **Source / scope:** `nng-heuristics`, `nng-complex`; general usability and expert
  complex applications respectively, not a universal visual style.

## Recognition, consistency and control

- **Principle / problem:** Make current state, available actions and consequences
  recognizable; reduce recall burden and allow recovery from slips.
- **Use:** Navigation, repeated controls, status, reversible edits and learned workflows.
- **Do not use:** Hide necessary complexity merely to reduce visible elements;
  consistency is not a reason to preserve a harmful pattern.
- **Alternatives:** Progressive disclosure, contextual help, history, preview or undo.
- **Exceptions:** Expert shortcuts complement a discoverable primary path. A novel
  interaction can be justified by evidence and clear feedback.
- **Accessibility:** Same function has consistent naming; meaningful sequence and
  visible focus support recognition beyond visual styling.
- **Source / scope:** `nng-heuristics`, `wcag22`; usability heuristics and relevant
  consistent-identification/interaction requirements.

## Resolve evidence by scope

- **Principle / problem:** Separate standards, research, observed artifacts and
  aesthetic preference so local recommendations do not become false laws.
- **Use:** Conflicting systems, platform units, uncertain findings, adopted patterns.
- **Do not use:** Citation count or a famous brand as proof of fit.
- **Alternatives:** State a hypothesis, test comparable tasks, ask the designer.
- **Exceptions:** Explicit user taste leads visual choices; describe remaining
  accessibility consequences accurately rather than claiming compliance.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG AA requirements are not interchangeable with AAA or
  platform recommendations; Figma annotations do not prove implemented behavior.
- **Source / scope:** Original synthesis of `wcag22`, `apple-accessibility`,
  `gov-questions`, `nng-complex`. Example: one-question pages help some services;
  expert comparison may need simultaneous fields. Apple points are not CSS pixels.
