# Form / service / transaction adequacy

Use when people supply information, make a consequential request, or complete a transaction.

## Jobs and connected expectations

- Explain purpose, eligibility, requirements, consequences, and expected effort.
- Ask understandable questions in a sequence that follows user knowledge.
- Preserve entered information and support correction, review, and recovery.
- Make validation, errors, saving, timeout, permissions, and alternative paths clear.
- Confirm what happened, what happens next, and how to get help.

Choose one-page, stepped, conversational, or review-led structure by complexity,
risk, dependencies, and return behavior. A field styling request remains a field
styling request. A complete service flow must make the consequential sequence and
recovery credible, not merely show a perfect form.

Common shallow failures: asking for data before explaining why; placeholder-only
labels; errors detached from fields; destructive submission without review where
review matters; fake progress; lost valid answers; confirmation with no next step;
checkout conventions applied to non-commerce services without evidence.

Enough means a person can understand, complete, check, submit, recover, and know the
outcome within the requested boundary. Do not invent policy, eligibility, legal
consent, identity requirements, payment behavior, or retention rules.
