# Editorial / reading adequacy

Use when understanding, finding, following, or resuming content is the main job.

## Jobs and connected expectations

- Set expectation: subject, voice, authorship, currency, and reading commitment.
- Support comprehension through hierarchy, measure, pacing, media, captions, and notes.
- Help people find a section, related item, or place to resume when the content warrants it.
- Preserve source, attribution, and citation relationships where credibility depends on them.
- Make collection-to-article and article-to-next transitions clear when included.

Architecture may be a linear essay, navigable chapters, annotated media, a magazine
collection, or reference documentation. A single article request need not grow into
a publication. A publication experience requires credible collection, article, and
wayfinding behavior rather than one stylized text block.

Common shallow failures: display typography overpowering reading; identical rhythm
for every content type; line length chosen from viewport convenience; decoration
breaking semantic sequence; related content with no editorial logic; no indication
of progress or return in long material.

Enough means the requested content can be understood at realistic length and the
reader can orient, navigate, and continue as the brief requires. Do not invent
authors, citations, publication history, or endorsements as facts.
