# Dashboard / data adequacy

Use when people monitor, compare, diagnose, or decide from measures and records.

## Jobs and connected expectations

- Establish the decision, audience, time horizon, units, denominators, and freshness.
- Show current state and meaningful comparison, change, threshold, or exception.
- Let people move from overview to the evidence or records behind a signal when
  diagnosis or action is part of the request.
- Keep filters, scope, selection, and applied state visible.
- Represent missing, delayed, partial, uncertain, and permission-limited data honestly.

Choose chart, table, narrative, alert, scorecard, or mixed composition by analytical
question. A dashboard is not a compulsory card grid. For an overview-only request,
the overview may be enough. For a decision workspace, include the connected route
to explanation or action needed to make the displayed signal useful.

Common shallow failures: unlabeled totals; gauges without targets; percentages
without denominator; decoration masquerading as data; every metric equally loud;
filters that hide scope; unexplained color; drill-down implied but not designed.

Enough means a person can interpret the measures and make the requested decision
without guessing provenance or state. Do not fabricate data confidence, causal
meaning, thresholds, alerts, or actions.
