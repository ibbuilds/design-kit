# Proportion, rhythm, and spacing craft

Use when relationships feel cramped, loose, monotonous, fragmented, or mechanically
regular. Spacing expresses structure and tempo; it is not a cleanup layer.

## Relational spacing

- Separate scales: internal component space, space between related items, section
  transitions, and large compositional voids. Each should make the hierarchy easier
  to perceive.
- Derive repeated values from a small useful family, then allow optical exceptions
  for type, icons, irregular shapes, and deliberate art direction. Token consistency
  does not require equal-looking mathematical gaps.
- Pair proportion with content. Image aspect, text measure, column width, control
  height, and surrounding space should reinforce the same priority.
- Create rhythm through recurrence and change: stable baselines or row cadence where
  comparison matters; longer pauses at conceptual transitions; tighter sequences
  where the user reads a unit as one thought.
- Evaluate density by task. Expert work may need compact, stable scanning; editorial
  or expressive surfaces may use expansion and silence. Neither density nor whitespace
  is quality by itself.

## Inspect

When every section has similar visual weight, compress secondary proof and increase
scale contrast between primary moments. Use asymmetry only where content supports it;
reinspect macro-rhythm before local spacing. When unrelated controls look grouped,
increase their separation relative to within-group gaps, then check scan order.
When density is excessive, expose content relationships before adding blanket padding.
When density is too sparse, tighten repeated units while preserving conceptual pauses.

Check edge padding, group gaps, repeated rows, baseline relationships, fold/viewport
transitions, and the balance of occupied versus quiet regions. Look for accidental
near-equalities that suggest a false relationship and abrupt gap changes with no
content reason. Test long labels, sparse and full states, and representative data.

Correct the governing relationship before nudging isolated pixels. Batch related
spacing changes, render the affected composition, and retain optical exceptions that
visibly help. Do not impose a universal base unit, golden ratio, or density target.
