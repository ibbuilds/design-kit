# Finish intelligence

For high visual ambition, substantial expressive work, or a credible hypothesis that
a resolved design lacks an important craft layer. Not a mandatory final phase.
Narrow shadow/control edits use the affected craft and current render directly.
These are aesthetic heuristics, revised 2026-09-05; no premium-style template or score.

**Correct** meets content, usability and construction needs. **Resolved** has coherent
hierarchy and a deliberate visual language. **Exceptionally finished** expresses that
language with distinctive, content-led focal moments and precise relationships from
composition to detail, including mobile. Lack of defects alone does not establish it.
A flat, monochrome or image-free design can satisfy all three.

## Find the consequential gap

Depth is a relationship among plane count, occlusion, scale, perspective, material
contrast and light; shadows are one cue. Choose the spatial logic before tuning its
effects. For deliberately flat work, resolve overlap, edges, type, contrast and rhythm
on that basis. Finish amplifies the thesis; a missing signature or weak product reveal
may require recomposition rather than more surface treatment.

From the actual render, what single credible intervention would most improve perception
while preserving the thesis? **None** is valid. Name the visible location/relationship,
why it matters, and the expected gain; a general wish for “more premium” is not a hypothesis.
This can reveal an opportunity despite correct content and geometry. Choose among the
implicated relationships below, not a checklist to complete. If visual material could
carry the gain, [asset direction](assets.md) investigates that specific opportunity.

| Relationship to resolve | Contextual judgment and rendered check |
|---|---|
| Depth and light | Establish which planes touch, lift, recede or occlude and why. Contact shadows anchor; ambient shadows separate; cast shadows imply light direction/distance; inset shading suggests recess; highlights describe edges. Use only needed cues. Shared scenes need coherent light and material behavior, not identical shadow values. Compare near/far surfaces and surrounding background at actual size. |
| Surface and edge | Tune thin-border hierarchy, opacity, inner highlight, adjacent tints, nested radii and corner transitions. Check anti-aliasing, seams, translucent overlaps, blur and texture at output scale. Grain must support material, not dirty the text field. Weaker shadows or fewer borders may improve separation. |
| Microtypography | Inspect optical rather than box alignment, baselines, leading/tracking, punctuation/quotes, headline measure and rag, avoidable widows/orphans, comparable numbers/units, label case/density and muted-text readability. Use [typography](typography.md) for the implicated relationship; do not swap an approved face to manufacture novelty. |
| Icons and controls | Match optical size, stroke weight, baseline, arrow position and internal padding. Raised/pressed edges must agree with the surface system. Check distinguishable selected/disabled/focus states and usable targets when in scope; hover cannot be the only cue on touch. Use [interaction](interaction-motion.md) for behavior, without inventing unseen states. |
| Compositing and signature | Check masks, edge halos, lighting, image/type overlap, semantic crop and foreground/background hierarchy. A recurring fragment, framing rule or transition should express this content, not stamp unrelated decoration onto every section. Native UI accuracy and editability remain part of finish. |
| Motion and responsiveness | Preserve cause, anchor and continuity; one meaningful transition can outperform many entrances. Still frames do not prove motion. At mobile scale reconsider crop, shadow muddiness, texture frequency, overlay clearance, readable type and affordances. Preserve the idea through recomposition, not miniature effects. |

## Evidence and intervention

For substantial new designs, finish includes [system integrity](../design-foundations.md#refine-the-system-and-artifact):
inspect whether semantic controls are instances, colors/type still consume their
definitions, and mobile shares the same sources. Judge flexibility with real content
and resizing. Correct drift at its source while preserving expressive one-off moments;
neither token coverage nor component count proves visual quality.

Let visible gaps drive [targeted retrieval](../intelligence.md): if a product frame reads
as a pasted rectangle where the thesis needs spatial integration, search framing/layering
relationships, not generic software landings. Inspect the detail and its context, apply
or reject the mechanism, then reinspect the affected composition. Retrieve only when
evidence would change this decision. A small or obstructed preview can establish massing
without proving edges, subtle light or microtype; use a permitted detail or retain the
limitation. Motion-dependent expression needs actual sequence evidence.

For a materially selected mechanism, did its useful relationship survive into the render?
Three overlapping planes separated by occlusion are not transferred by putting a shadow
under one rectangle. Compare causal relationships at readable scale, not literal layouts
or effect counts. Preserve purposeful adaptation and intentional flatness; do not import
depth when the thesis needs typographic precision. Shared labels never prove transfer.

Try the highest-leverage supported intervention with the smallest useful scope; compare a fresh equivalent viewport
before/after and, where useful, with the effect/asset removed. Inspect isolated detail
AND the complete composition. Keep only a visible gain in meaning, hierarchy,
identity, usability or intentional atmosphere. Preserve purposeful density and human
choices. Never pay for polish with weaker comprehension or accessible state cues.
Use [quality's stopping rule](../quality.md): pursue credible improvements, stop
insignificant taste churn and respect the user's stopping decision.
