## UI/UX process and AI design context — September 30, 2026

The user's constraints set the goal (exceptional design, low total effort, chosen agent, human supervision); they do not establish a scientifically optimal method. This revision independently compares professional responsibilities and AI-design findings, then applies only what changes design work. Research and structural coverage are not proof of aesthetic improvement or measured model savings.

| Source checked | Supported design practice / implementation | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Design Council: Framework for Innovation](https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/our-resources/framework-for-innovation) | Understand the problem, define, explore and refine; iterate rather than freeze a one-way sequence. ONBOARDING.md now resolves experience structure before dependent visual expansion and retains local returns. | Broad design framework; not a model benchmark or a mandatory artifact inventory. |
| [GOV.UK: user needs](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/start-by-learning-user-needs) | Use actual needs/evidence and context, distinguish assumptions from observed user knowledge. Applied in context/BRIEF.md. | Government-service guidance adapted proportionally; no invented personas or mandatory research stage for every small edit. |
| [NN/g: IA versus navigation](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ia-vs-navigation) and [GOV.UK: interface writing](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/writing-for-user-interfaces) | Content grouping, terminology and task structure precede decoration; clear copy/labels are part of design. Applied in experience phase, TASTE.md and DESIGN_DIRECTION.md. | Do not impose government tone or appearance; content-heavy sites and small screens require different scope. |
| [GOV.UK: prototypes](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/making-prototypes) | Choose fidelity for the unresolved design question; prototype before expensive expansion. Preserve useful layout but harden/replace prototype logic for production. | A prototype is not automatically production-safe; the GOV.UK kit is not required. |
| [NN/g: UX-context design, July 2026](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ux-context-design/) | Curate product-specific design knowledge and usage rules as narrow context beside the work; evaluate its actual contribution. Applied in the shared record and selective routes. | The article names open research questions; it does not establish this kit, a fixed context size or Sol savings. |
| [NN/g: vague prototyping](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/vague-prototyping) and [evaluation methodology](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/testing-ai-methodology) | Specific tasks, hierarchy, representative content and lightweight visual inputs reduce ambiguity; inspect design through the task, not just polish. Applied in comparative refinement. | Tested contexts/tools differ from the user's model. Clearer inputs do not guarantee exceptional outputs. |

The independent correction is concrete: context/need, experience structure and design comparison are explicit responsibilities, not assumed to emerge from a palette or component library. The default contribution is UI/UX; the agent owns relevant artifact QA inside the authorized batch, while formal technical audits/release requirements are conditional on their requested scope. Research sessions, accessibility conformance studies, analytics and release operations remain available when actually needed/requested, not compulsory checkpoints. Human approval authorizes expansion; it does not establish usability research findings.
