# Taste

User-authorized interpretation of their statements and supplied library. Reuse it
without another taste interview. Judge the actual interface by these criteria:

- **Functional depth:** complete the real job with the needed information, controls
  and states. Choose density and complexity for the task.
- **Purposeful restraint:** every element supports understanding, action, identity
  or feedback. Remove waste while preserving useful capability and expression.
- **Meticulous polish:** deliberate type, composition, spacing, copy and assets;
  resolved wrapping, crops and responsive priorities with realistic content.
- **Seamless behavior:** predictable actions, timely feedback, continuity, loading,
  errors and recovery. Motion explains or enriches without obstructing the job.
- **Distinctive coherence:** recognizable product identity and consistent visual
  and interaction logic appropriate to its audience.
- **Durable quality:** useful, accessible and maintainable beyond trends; adapt
  when needs change. No sparse-layout mandate, prescribed decade or futuristic theme.

Translate these into project decisions within creative authority. Verify inferred
choices in the render; do not turn them into permanent user preferences.

The [user's library](../references/product-references.md) is read on demand. Prefer
relevant user research, applicable standards and production patterns before aesthetic
inspiration. An index or extracted DESIGN.md is secondary evidence; verify important
values and behavior at the source. No universal palette, font or brand is selected.
