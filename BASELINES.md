# Preserve accepted quality

- Keep accepted baselines in the target using existing tools.
- Record acceptance, revision, content/state, viewport, browser, fonts and assets.
- Capture important responsive and interaction states in context.
- Preserve expected behavior: focus, input, motion and task completion.
- Compare equivalent conditions. Check hierarchy, wrapping, crops, density, behavior and perceived speed.
- Separate rendering noise from regressions.
- Update for accepted improvements with before/after evidence; never approve a regression just to pass.
