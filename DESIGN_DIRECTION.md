# Visual direction through section images

Use this selective visual-critique aid under [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). Aesthetic explanations are not substitutes for actually viewing the generated images.

## Establish what is stable and what is still open

The user-owned foundation and the selected first-section image are the main anchors. Preserve explicitly approved identity, copy, assets, accessibility requirements and visual treatments. Do not infer that every incidental detail in one generated image is a design-system rule. Note whether each relationship is user-confirmed, agent-proposed or unresolved.

One section can be compositionally different from another while sharing type, color roles, geometry, density and image direction. Mechanical repetition of identical cards is not the same as coherence.

## Diagnose visual problems at the right scale

| Observed image gap | Substantial intervention |
| --- | --- |
| Indistinct focal point | Rework region proportions, emphasis order and type/image competition |
| Generic hero or interchangeable section | Reframe product-specific asset, composition, content hierarchy and art direction |
| Flat page pacing | Give individual sections purpose-built compositions and negative-space rhythms |
| Later section drifts from the first | Reintroduce the chosen image as reference; correct typography, color, surface or visual density as a group |
| Good typography but poor grouping | Recompose major content regions rather than nudging margins |
| Unusable mobile crop | Generate a separate mobile composition with preserved essential content |
| Attractive isolated images but weak full-page sequence | Compare adjacent selected images and revise the responsible section's structure |

Name a **visible hypothesis** before regenerating. Good moves alter the overall reading at normal image size. After structure is convincing, smaller craft refinements can complete it. Do not introduce an unrelated visual identity simply to make the revision larger.

## References help; they do not decide

The user's selected section images carry more authority than unrelated inspiration. Existing Awwwards and One Page Love MCPs can surface useful references for open visual questions. Inspect the actual example; transfer relationships, not its logo or product identity. An image reference cannot verify interactions or underlying tokens. [REFERENCE_ROUTER.md](REFERENCE_ROUTER.md) provides the existing scoped research workflow.

## Decide using images

Compare candidates at equivalent dimensions, content and viewing scale; check legibility, identity, focus, type/image balance, visual rhythm and the section's objective. Preserve stronger earlier versions; do not present unsupported rankings or model self-scores as approval.

Inspect the selected sections **together in page order** before calling the visual direction decided. Carry approved relationships into the handoff, note uncertain asset/text fidelity and responsive limits, and **stop before code or final editable design creation**.
